Bio

I am an Illustrator and Digital Artist originally from the Iowa town of Princeton. I received a B.F.A. in Media Arts and Animation from the Illinois Institute of Art — Chicago in 2008, where I began my professional career at Calabash Animation, contributing to commercials for Trix, Lucky Charms, and other brands — growing from the 2D clean-up crew to a lead artist. Since then, life has taken me through Louisiana, Wisconsin, and California, and I now call Colorado home. In my free time, I find myself drawing caricatures, perfecting my portraiture skills, and spending time with my family.

I am a creative problem solver who loves learning new skills while improving the old. I have contributed to projects for Amazon, Dolby, General Mills, Unilever, McDonalds, Radio Flyer, and Gatorade.
